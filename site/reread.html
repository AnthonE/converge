    How saving works

    Each answer is kept on this phone as soon as you tap it. “Save to the map” opens GitHub with your answers written into a new file; tap the green Commit changes button, and again in the box that opens. The site rebuilds in a few minutes and shows the pages as re-read by a person. Answers leave this phone’s list once the site has them.

    If GitHub opens in its app instead of the browser, press and hold “Save to the map” and choose to open it in the browser. Or tap “Copy my answers” and paste them to Claude.
