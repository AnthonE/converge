The Ideals Map private prototype

What the movements around made minds say they serve, how they link, and which conditions are met. Start with the heart: what the traditions saw.

    • Ideal: the end being served
    • Carrier: a movement, group or public voice
    • Vehicle: the made thing it runs on
    • Event: something documented, reported or alleged
    • Text: a tradition's own words
    • Triangle: a funder from the money view who is also tied to an ideal
    • Phase condition: met, partly met, not met, unknown
    • Dotted link: a way of reading, not a finding
    • Offshoot: a group that came out of a carrier's milieu, shown with its public record. Not an ideal
    • Funder: a person, foundation, fund, firm or government
    • AI company
    • Evaluator: a body that tests, ranks or scores; most here test AI models
    • Circle: a name from the ideals map, a venue or another recipient
    • Vehicle from the ideals map
    • Event: something a venue hosted
    • Solid line: a record of money (a gift, grant, investment, loan, pledge or commission)
    • Long-dash line: hosts or runs
    • Dash-dot line: evaluates
    • Ideal: what a movement serves
    • How it takes hold: a mechanism named in scholarship
    • Ancient pattern, with its sources
    • Solid line: a carrier uses the ancient name itself
    • Dotted line: this project's reading
    • Dashed line: a recorded use of a mechanism (documented or reported)

    Everything on the map, as a list

    Movements are described in their own words where those were found, and otherwise by published reporting and by observers named as such. The names of the ideals are this map's own. Nothing here diagnoses a person. A parallel text is a lens: it can motivate a question and never confirms a source. A record of money is not a finding about anyone's work or motive.
